## Prep and thaw schedule
- Monday evening: move one frozen salmon serving to the refrigerator for Tuesday. Keep the second frozen.
- Tuesday: use the first prepared breakfast/lunch and cook the barbecue salmon. When buying chicken, keep Wednesday's batch refrigerated and freeze Thursday's batch if following the two-day refrigerator routine.
- Wednesday: use the second prepared breakfast/lunch. Cook two servings of honey-garlic chicken; pack Thursday lunch. Make two jars of chia overnight oats for Thursday and Friday. Move Thursday chicken to the refrigerator to thaw.
- Thursday: breakfast and lunch are ready. Cook two servings of barbecue chicken and pack Friday lunch. Move the remaining salmon to the refrigerator to thaw for Friday.
- Friday: breakfast and lunch are ready. Cook the teriyaki salmon bowl. Use remaining cucumber and avocado alongside lunch/dinner; leave extra edamame frozen.
